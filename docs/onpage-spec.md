**FAQ**

- **How much is California income tax?**
  California has 9 tax brackets for a single filer, from 1% to 12.3%.
- **What is the top California income tax rate?**
  The highest California rate is 12.3%, which applies above $742,953.
- **Does California tax self-employment income?**
  Yes. California taxes net self-employment profit as ordinary income, on top of the 15.3% federal self-employment tax.
- **Are there local income taxes in California?**
