Every calculator on the site is listed here, grouped by the question it answers. Each
one links to the next question people usually have, so you can follow a decision from
"what does this place cost" to "what would I keep there" without starting over.

## Every calculator on the site

The place calculators cover the cost of living in a metro area, a side-by-side
comparison of two places, and what rent an income can carry. The income calculators
convert between salary and hourly pay, compare salaries across cities, and work out
the salary a place requires. The tax calculators cover the paycheck and take-home pay,
federal and state income tax, sales tax and property tax. The housing calculators
cover what home price an income supports, the full monthly payment on it, and the
cash needed at closing.

## Which one answers your question

- Weighing a job offer in another state: the paycheck calculator for that state, then
  the cost of living comparison.
- Deciding whether you can afford a city: the living wage calculator, then rent
  affordability.
- Planning to buy: home affordability first, then the house payment and closing cost
  calculators with the price it gives you.

## How these tools fit together

The calculators share their engines, so a figure computed on one page is the same
figure on the next: the paycheck for a state, the tax inside a living wage, and the
tax side of a cost-of-living comparison all come from the same tax calculation, and
all the rent figures come from the same official rents.
