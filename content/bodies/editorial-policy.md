This site publishes calculators and the explanations around them. Both are held to
the same rule: a number that cannot be traced to a statute, an official dataset or a
tested calculation is not published.

## How content is written

Each page is written for one question — the one its calculator answers — and explains
how the answer is reached, what changes it, and what it leaves out. Figures in the
text are taken from the same engines that run the calculators, not estimated
separately, so the explanation and the result cannot disagree. Hypothetical examples
are labelled as examples.

## How it is reviewed

Before a page is published, every figure in its text is checked against a register
that records what each number rests on: a calculation with a named test, a value in
one of our datasets, a cited statute, or a named official publication. A figure with
no entry in that register stops the page from being published. Statistics from
outside sources are checked against the source itself before launch and again when
the source is updated.

## Use of machine assistance

Explanatory text on this site is drafted with the help of an AI writing assistant and
then edited and checked by the author, H.Hemati. No figure on the site is produced by
a language model: every number comes from a calculation engine, a dataset or a cited
source, and passes the review above.

## Corrections policy

When a figure or statement is wrong, it is corrected in the underlying calculation or
data, and the page records the correction and its date. Corrections are not made
silently. If you believe something here is wrong, the contact page explains how to
report it.
