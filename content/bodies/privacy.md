This page explains what this site stores, what it measures, and the choices you have.

## What stays in your browser

The calculators run in your browser. The salary, rent, prices and other figures you
enter are not sent to us, are not stored on our servers, and are not attached to you
in any way. Closing the page discards them.

## What we measure

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — which pages are visited,
which calculators are used, and which links people follow from one tool to the next.
These measurements record the category of an action, such as "a paycheck was
calculated for an hourly wage", and never the figures you entered. Analytics uses
cookies or similar technology, subject to the choices described below.

## Advertising and cookies

This site is funded by advertising served by Google. Third-party vendors, including
Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other
websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve
ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you
can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising
through aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its
services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Your choices

- **Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland** are
  asked for consent before advertising or analytics cookies are used, through a
  consent tool certified by Google, and can change that choice at any time from the
  link in the footer.
- **Visitors in US states with consumer privacy laws**, including California, can use
  the "Do not sell or share my personal information" link in the footer to opt out of
  personalised advertising.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser; the calculators work without them.

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact
page.
