LifeCalc Pro answers two questions that are usually asked on two different sites: what
does it cost to live in a place, and what would you actually keep there. The
calculators here put both halves side by side — cost of living from official price
indices, and take-home pay from the tax rules of the state you would be living in.

## Why the numbers come from primary sources

Cost of living comes from the Bureau of Economic Analysis and the Department of
Housing and Urban Development; tax comes from the Internal Revenue Code, the IRS and
each state's revenue department. These are the sources the figures ultimately rest
on, they are free to cite, and anyone can check a number against them. The
methodology page explains each calculation, and the sources page lists every source.

## How this site makes money

The site is free to use and is funded by advertising. Advertisers do not choose,
review or influence any calculation or any text, and no page recommends a product.
Ads are never placed between a calculator and its result.
