North Carolina moved from graduated brackets to a single flat rate more than a decade
ago and has been lowering that rate in steps since. For 2026 it is **3.99%**, applied
after a **$12,750 standard deduction** for a single filer, with no personal exemption
and no local income tax in any city or county. A paycheck from Charlotte and one from
Asheville on the same salary are identical.

## $95,000 in North Carolina

The state tax is 3.99% of the salary less the standard deduction: **$3,282** for the
year. After federal tax, Social Security and Medicare, a single filer keeps
**$72,381**, an effective 23.8%.

| Paid | Each check |

| Every two weeks (26) | about $2,784 |
| Twice a month (24) | about $3,016 |

| Weekly (52) | about $1,392 |

## Why the standard deduction matters more than the rate

At a flat rate, the deduction does most of the work of making the tax progressive.
The first $12,750 of wages carries no state tax at all, so the effective state rate
climbs slowly toward 3.99% as income rises and never reaches it. On $95,000 the state
takes less than 3.5% of gross. A **north carolina income tax calculator** that simply
multiplies salary by the headline rate overstates the tax by the value of that
deduction.

## A rate that keeps changing

North Carolina's rate is set by statute year by year, and recent legislation has
scheduled further reductions tied to revenue conditions. When the rate changes, the
state line on a paycheck changes with it, while the federal and FICA lines do not.
This calculator uses the rate in force for 2026; check the year shown with any figure
you rely on.

## Hourly and overtime pay

Overtime above 40 hours a week is paid at one and a half times the regular rate, and
North Carolina taxes it at the same 3.99% as regular pay — with no brackets, extra
hours cannot push you into a higher state rate. A **north carolina paycheck tax
calculator** for hourly work only has to build gross pay correctly; the state line
follows directly.

## Deductions taken through payroll

A traditional 401(k) contribution lowers both federal and North Carolina taxable
wages, since the state follows the federal exclusion. Health premiums deducted before
tax lower Social Security and Medicare as well.

## Form NC-4

North Carolina has its own withholding certificate, Form NC-4, and an employer uses it
rather than your federal W-4 to decide the state withholding on each check. A new job,
a second job or a change in household is the moment to update it — otherwise the flat
rate is applied to the wrong base and the difference turns up in April.
