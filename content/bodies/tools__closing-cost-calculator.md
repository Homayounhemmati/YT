Closing costs are the part of buying a home that people underestimate, because
they are quoted as a percentage and paid as a lump sum. Two to five per cent of
the purchase price sounds manageable until it is $14,000 due on a specific
Thursday.

## How closing costs are calculated

There is no single formula, because closing costs are a stack of separate charges
from separate parties. They fall into four groups:

**Lender fees** — origination, underwriting, application, credit report. Negotiable
in principle, and the group most worth comparing between lenders.

**Third-party services** — appraisal, home inspection, survey, title search, title
insurance. Largely fixed by the market rather than the lender, though the title
policy can sometimes be shopped.

**Prepaid items and escrow** — the first year of homeowner's insurance, prepaid
interest to the end of the month, and several months of property tax paid into
escrow up front. **This group is the one people miss**, because it is not a fee at
all. It is money you would have paid anyway, collected earlier.

**Government charges** — recording fees and transfer taxes. These vary enormously
by state and city and are not negotiable.

```
cash to close = closing costs + down payment − any seller credits
```

**Cash to close is the number that matters**, and it is not the same as closing
costs. A **cash to close calculator** that reports only the fees leaves out the
largest line on the page.

## Why the range is so wide

Transfer taxes explain most of it. Some states charge nothing; others charge a
percentage of the purchase price that can run into five figures on a median home.
Two identical purchases in two states can differ by more than the appraisal, the
inspection and the origination fee combined.

Who pays what is also regional custom rather than law. In some markets the seller
customarily pays the owner's title policy; in others the buyer does. It is
negotiable everywhere and assumed in most places.

## What this does not include

- The down payment, which is separate from closing costs even though it is due at
  the same moment.
- Moving costs, immediate repairs and furnishing.
- HOA transfer and capitalisation fees, which apply only to some properties and
  can be substantial.
- Seller concessions, which are negotiated per transaction and cannot be estimated
  in advance.

## Who needs this

Anyone asking **how much are closing costs** early enough to do something about the
answer — which means before making an offer, not after. The figure changes how much
house is affordable, because money spent at closing is money not available for the
down payment, and the down payment sets the loan-to-value ratio and therefore the
rate.

The Loan Estimate a lender must provide within three business days of application
is the authoritative version of this number. A **buyer closing cost calculator** is
for the stage before that, when you are deciding whether to apply at all.
