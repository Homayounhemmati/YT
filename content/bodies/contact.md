## How to reach us

Use the contact form for anything about the site: a question about how a figure was
calculated, a state or city you would like to see covered, or a problem with how a
page works on your device. We do not offer personal tax or financial advice, and
messages asking for it will be answered with a pointer to the relevant calculator and
its methodology rather than an opinion on your circumstances.

## Reporting an incorrect number

If a figure looks wrong, tell us the page, the figure, and what you expected it to be
— and if you are comparing it with an official source, which one. Every number on the
site traces to a calculation, a dataset or a cited source, so a report can be checked
precisely. Confirmed errors are corrected in the calculation or data, and the page
records the correction.

## Response times

We aim to reply within a few working days. Reports of an incorrect figure are handled
first, because a wrong number is the one thing a calculator site cannot leave
standing.
