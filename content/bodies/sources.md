Every number on this site traces to one of the sources below. If you find a figure
that does not match its source, the last section says how to tell us.

## Federal tax sources

- **Internal Revenue Code**, Title 26 of the United States Code — in particular
  section 1 (tax rates), 63 (standard deduction), 1401 and 1402 (self-employment tax),
  3101 (the employee's Social Security and Medicare tax) and 199A (the qualified
  business income deduction).
- **IRS annual inflation adjustments**, which set each year's brackets and standard
  deduction.
- **Social Security Administration**, for each year's Social Security wage base.

## State tax sources

Each state's figures carry their own citations — the statute section and the revenue
department's current-year instructions or withholding tables — and every state page
lists those citations directly beneath its calculator. State figures are drawn from a
maintained open-source model of state tax law and checked against those primary
sources before the page is published.

## Cost-of-living sources

- **Bureau of Economic Analysis**, Regional Price Parities by state and metropolitan
  area.
- **Department of Housing and Urban Development**, Fair Market Rents by county and
  metro area.
- For the rules quoted in explanations — the federal cost-burden definitions, the
  Homeowners Protection Act, the Fair Labor Standards Act — the statute or regulation
  is named where it is quoted.

## How to report an error

If a figure on this site disagrees with its source, use the contact page and include
the page, the figure, and the source you are comparing it with. Corrections are made
in the calculation or the data, never by editing the displayed number, and a
corrected page says what changed and when.
